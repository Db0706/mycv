My Routine

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

My Routine is published by the developer of My Routine ("we", "us"). You can contact us at contact@deandev10.info.

The short version

We do not collect your data. My Routine has no accounts and no servers that hold your information. What you enter stays on your iPhone and, if iCloud is turned on, in your own private iCloud storage, which we cannot see.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores the routines you add (names, form, amount, schedule and notes), what you record (taken, skipped, and the times you record), and your settings.

  • On your iPhone. This data is kept in the app's storage on your device and is included in your normal iPhone backups.
  • In your iCloud (optional). If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is allowed for My Routine, Apple syncs this data to your private iCloud database so it appears on your other devices. Apple stores it under your Apple Account. We have no access to it.

Routine names and records can say something about your health. That is why the app keeps them on your devices and in your own iCloud, rather than on our systems.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled by your iPhone itself. They are not sent through our servers. By default a reminder only says "Routine reminder: You have an item scheduled." Item names are only shown if you turn on "Show item names" in Settings, in which case they may be visible on your lock screen.

Calendar (optional)

If you turn on "Add to Calendar", the app asks for permission to use Calendar and creates a separate calendar called "My Routine". It only writes to that calendar and does not read or change your other calendars or events. Event titles do not include item names unless you choose to include them. If your calendar account syncs (for example through iCloud or Google), those events sync with it under that provider's terms.

Purchases

My Routine is free to use with one routine. An optional one-off purchase unlocks unlimited routines. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account; we never see your name, email or payment details.

To know whether the unlock has been bought, the app asks Apple on your iPhone. That check stays between your device and Apple. We only receive Apple's sales reports, which show totals and do not identify you. Deleting your data in the app does not affect your purchase, which stays with your Apple Account.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party tracking code.
  • We do not sell or share your data, because we do not have it.
  • We do not provide medical advice.

Your choices and rights

  • Export: Settings → Export my data creates a file with everything the app stores.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete all data removes everything from your iPhone and from your iCloud copy. Deleting the app removes the data on that device. You can also manage My Routine's iCloud data in the iPhone Settings app: tap your name at the top, then iCloud.
  • Notifications and Calendar access can be turned off at any time in iPhone Settings.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have a question about your privacy, email contact@deandev10.info. If you are in the UK, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

My Routine is not directed at children under 13.

Health disclaimer

This application helps you record and remember routines you enter. It does not provide medical advice or replace instructions from a qualified healthcare professional.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
