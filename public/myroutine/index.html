My Routine support

My Routine helps you record the medications and supplements you already take, reminds you at the times you set, and lets you record each one with a tap. It is free.

Email: contact@deandev10.info

Reminders are not arriving

  • Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → My Routine and check that notifications are allowed.
  • Check whether a Focus mode (such as Sleep or Do Not Disturb) is hiding them.
  • Open My Routine once. Opening the app refreshes the upcoming reminders.
  • In the app, Settings → Send a test notification shows a test within a few seconds.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone and, if iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud storage. We have no servers holding your data and cannot see it. See the Privacy Policy.

Moving to a new iPhone

If iCloud is on for My Routine, sign in with the same Apple Account on the new iPhone and install the app. Your routines appear after a short while. A normal iPhone backup and restore also brings them across.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings → Delete all data removes everything from your iPhone and from iCloud. Settings → Export my data saves a copy first if you want one.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

Important

This application helps you record and remember routines you enter. It does not provide medical advice or replace instructions from a qualified healthcare professional.
